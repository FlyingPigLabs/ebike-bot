# Changes: other-vehicle-types.pdf (Arizona Vehicle Type Definitions — A.R.S. §28-101)

Checked against: https://www.azleg.gov/ars/28/00101.htm and https://www.azleg.gov/ars/28/00601.htm
(fetched 11/27/2026; the server says the 28-101 page was last modified 9/9/2026, so the 2026 session
renumbering — e-bike at ¶29, motor vehicle at ¶53, etc. — is already in). Also checked §44-1641, §28-5201,
§28-1225, §28-9501 and §28-9503 for the cross-references.

## Result of the check
- Every ¶ number in the draft matches the current §28-101 (6, 3, 5, 9, 11, 13, 17, 29, 30, 31, 32, 35, 37, 38,
  43, 44, 45, 49, 50, 51, 52, 53, 55, 56, 57, 59, 63, 64, 65, 66, 72, 75, 76, 81, 84, 88, 89, 90, 91).
  §28-601(16) pole trailer is also correct.
- No summary was wrong on a number, weight, speed, wattage or wheel count. Every change below fills a gap
  or tightens wording.
- Motorized skateboard / motorized wheelchair: the citations ¶53(b)(i)/(ii) are right. §28-101 has no paragraph
  of its own for either term. Both are defined inside the motor vehicle exclusion: "For the purposes of this subdivision:
  (i) 'Motorized skateboard' means a self-propelled device that does not have handlebars and that has a motor, a deck on
  which a person may ride and at least two tandem wheels in contact with the ground. (ii) 'Motorized wheelchair' means a
  self-propelled wheelchair that is used by a person for mobility." Motorized wheelchair is defined again, word for word,
  in ¶63 and in §28-601(11) ("any self-propelled wheelchair that is used by a person for mobility"). Motorized
  skateboard is not defined in §28-601.

## Changes (old -> new)

1. Header: "accessed 11/18/2026" -> "accessed 11/27/2026".

2. Electric miniature scooter ¶30
   Old: "max speed not over 10 mph on a paved level surface."
   New: "max speed not over 10 mph, with or without human propulsion, on a paved level surface."
   Statute ¶30(f): "Has a maximum speed that does not exceed ten miles per hour, with or without human propulsion, on a paved level surface."

3. Electric standup scooter ¶32
   Old: "max speed not over 20 mph on a paved level surface."
   New: "max speed not over 20 mph, with or without human propulsion, on a paved level surface."
   Statute ¶32(a)(vi): "...does not exceed twenty miles per hour, with or without human propulsion, on a paved level surface."

4. Motorized skateboard ¶53(b)(i). Citation kept. Added a scope note.
   Added: "Defined only within the ¶53(b) exclusion."
   Statute ¶53(b): "For the purposes of this subdivision: (i) 'Motorized skateboard' means ..."

5. Motorized wheelchair ¶53(b)(ii). Citation kept. Added the other places it is defined.
   Old: "Self-propelled wheelchair used by a person for mobility."
   New: "Self-propelled wheelchair used by a person for mobility (also ¶63, §28-601(11))."
   Statute ¶63: "For the purposes of this paragraph, 'motorized wheelchair' means a self-propelled wheelchair that is used by a person for mobility."
   §28-601(11): "'Motorized wheelchair' means any self-propelled wheelchair that is used by a person for mobility."

6. Personal mobile cargo carrying device ¶65
   Old: "Electrically powered; operated primarily on sidewalks and in crosswalks ...; designed to stay within 25 ft of the property owner"
   New: "Electronically powered; used primarily on sidewalks and in crosswalks ...; owner-monitored, designed to stay within 25 ft of the owner"
   Statute: "an electronically powered device that ... (d) Is equipped with technology to transport personal property with the active
   monitoring of a property owner and that is primarily designed to remain within twenty-five feet of the property owner."

7. Neighborhood electric shuttle ¶55
   Old: "vehicle for hire under §28-9503 permit ... Includes aftermarket modification to carry up to 15."
   New: "vehicle for hire (§28-9501) under §28-9503 permit ... Includes non-manufacturer aftermarket modification to carry up to 15."
   Statute ¶55(a)(v): "The vehicle is a vehicle for hire as defined in section 28-9501 and operates under a vehicle for hire company permit issued pursuant to section 28-9503."
   ¶55(b): "...modified after market and not by the manufacturer to transport up to fifteen passengers, including the driver."

8. Motorized quadricycle ¶52
   Old: "operable on motor alone; ... licensed manufacturer"
   New: "operable on flat ground on motor alone; ... licensed (ch. 10) manufacturer"
   Statute ¶52(d): "The vehicle is operable on a flat surface using solely the electric motor without assistance from the pedals or passengers."
   ¶52(g): "manufactured by a motor vehicle manufacturer that is licensed pursuant to chapter 10 of this title."

9. Off-road recreational motor vehicle ¶59: "mining or agriculture" -> "mining or agricultural purposes" (wording only).
   Statute: "...used for construction, building trade, mining or agricultural purposes."

10. Livery vehicle ¶45. Added subdivision (d), which separates livery from taxi.
    Old: "Seating not over 15 incl. driver; fare by flat rate or flat hourly rate between zones or within an area; available on exclusive or shared-ride basis."
    New: "Seating not over 15 incl. driver; flat-rate or flat-hourly fare between zones or within an area; exclusive or shared-ride hire;
          may run a regular route or offer prearranged (§28-141) or contracted on-demand service."
    Statute ¶45(d): "May do any of the following: (i) Operate on a regular route or between specified places. (ii) Offer prearranged ground
    transportation service as defined in section 28-141. (iii) Offer on demand ground transportation service pursuant to a contract with
    a public airport, licensed business entity or organization."

11. Taxi ¶81
    Old: "does not primarily operate on a regular route; ... or prearranged service at a predetermined fare."
    New: "does not primarily operate on a regular route or between specified places; ... or prearranged service (§28-141) at a predetermined fare."
    Statute ¶81(a): "Does not primarily operate on a regular route or between specified places." (b): "...prearranged ground transportation
    service as defined in section 28-141 for a predetermined fare."

12. Authorized emergency vehicle ¶5(d): added "in its sole discretion".
    Statute: "...authorized by the department in its sole discretion and that meets liability insurance requirements prescribed by the department."

13. Vehicle transporter ¶91
    Old: "...or one with a stinger-steered or securely fastened dolly-mounted fifth wheel able to do so."
    New: "...incl. one with a stinger-steered fifth wheel or a dolly-mounted fifth wheel fastened at 2+ points."
    Statute ¶91(b): "...a truck tractor with a dolly mounted fifth wheel that is securely fastened to the truck tractor at two or more points..."

14. Reading note 4 (§28-601)
    Old: "Chapter 3 (traffic and vehicle regulation) has additional definitions in §28-601, including pole trailer, motorized wheelchair and a
          separate definition of "truck." Scrap vehicle: see §44-1641. Commercial motor vehicle: see §28-5201."
    New: "Chapter 3 (traffic and vehicle regulation) has its own definitions in §28-601, incl. pole trailer (16), motorized wheelchair (11) and a
          separate "truck" (30). "Motorized skateboard" is defined only in the ¶53(b) exclusion. Scrap vehicle: §44-1641. Commercial motor vehicle: §28-5201."
    Statute: §28-601 "In this chapter, unless the context otherwise requires: ... 11. 'Motorized wheelchair' ... 16. 'Pole trailer' ...
    30. 'Truck' means a motor vehicle that is designed, used or maintained primarily for the transportation of property."
    §28-101 ¶74: "'Scrap vehicle' has the same meaning prescribed in section 44-1641." (§44-1641 defines it.) §28-5201(1) defines "commercial motor vehicle".
